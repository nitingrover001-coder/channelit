What is PPC?

Pay Per Click Advertising Explained

Pay Per Click (PPC) advertising is a digital marketing model where you pay a fee each time someone clicks on your ad. It is one of the fastest ways to drive targeted traffic to your website and generate qualified leads.

Unlike organic strategies that take time to build, PPC delivers immediate visibility on platforms like Google, Bing, Facebook, and Instagram. When done right, every dollar you invest can return multiples in revenue.
